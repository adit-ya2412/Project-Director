## Style override: stillness

This project uses the `stillness` style - long static holds, no camera motion, deliberate quiet. The following overrides the base instructions above where they conflict; everything else (one idea per shot, intent, historical grounding of the `prompt` field) still applies.

- **Camera: `static` is the default movement, not one option among seven.** The base intent→camera table above (reveal→pull_back, explain→static, contrast→slow_push, introduce→pan, compare→split_frame, emphasize→slow_zoom) is overridden outright - do not consult it. Every shot's camera defaults to `static` regardless of its intent.
- **Exactly one permitted move: `slow_push` at `intensity` 0.10 or below.** Use it sparingly, only on a shot the narration genuinely lingers on - a beat that earns the extra weight of a drift, not a rotation you apply every few shots. `punch_in`, `pull_back`, `slow_zoom`, `pan`, and `split_frame` are all off-limits for this style - they are the vocabulary of a style with momentum, and momentum is exactly what "no camera motion" rules out.
- **Transitions: `cut` and `dissolve` only.** `wipeleft`, `fadeblack`, and all three `glitch_*` transitions are off-limits here, with no exception for a structural beat - restraint is this style's whole thesis, and a showy transition undercuts it precisely where the base prompt's "genuinely deliberate structural beat" carve-out would otherwise tempt you.
- **Prefer the coarsest possible fragment granularity.** Combine fragments into a longer shot wherever they still hold together as one idea, rather than giving each fragment its own shot. This is the inverse of a fast style's instinct, and it is what makes the pacing band's lack of a floor actually usable - a shot is allowed to sit for as long as the idea justifies.
- **Text cards: near-never.** This style says less, not more. Leave `text_card` empty on the vast majority of shots; only a genuinely essential fact or date belongs on screen, and even then, prefer letting the narration and the image carry it alone.
