# ADR-008

## Title

Human-in-the-Loop Approval

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

Creative planning generated by AI may not always match the creator's intent.

Proceeding directly to media generation can lead to wasted API costs and incorrect outputs.

---

## Decision

The workflow introduces a mandatory human approval checkpoint after Timeline generation.

The creator reviews:

- Scene breakdown
- Shot planning
- Camera directions
- Estimated durations
- Asset strategies

The workflow proceeds only after approval.

---

## Consequences

Advantages

- Better creative control
- Lower generation costs
- Easier iteration
- Fewer unnecessary AI requests

Disadvantages

- Adds one manual approval step

---

## Future

Future versions may support:

- Auto-approval
- Confidence-based approval
- Partial approval
- Team review workflows
