# Creative Philosophy

> **Version:** 1.0
>
> This document defines the creative principles that guide every AI planning agent within the Video Generation Engine.
>
> Every planning decision should be traceable back to one or more principles defined here.

---

# Purpose

The goal of the Video Generation Engine is **not** to generate random AI videos.

Its purpose is to create videos that feel intentionally directed.

Every visual should reinforce the narration.

Every camera movement should have purpose.

Every shot should move the story forward.

The engine should think less like an image generator and more like a documentary filmmaker.

---

# Mission Statement

Transform written ideas into cinematic short-form videos by combining structured storytelling, intelligent visual planning, existing media assets, and AI-generated content while minimizing manual editing.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

We believe that:

> Story drives visuals.

Not the other way around.

Visuals exist to strengthen understanding.

They should never distract from the narrative.

---

# Core Principles

---

## Principle 1 — Narrative First

The narration is the source of truth.

Visuals exist only to reinforce what is currently being said.

Every shot must answer one question:

> "Does this help the viewer better understand the narration?"

If the answer is no, the shot should not exist.

---

## Principle 2 — Visual Reinforcement

Every sentence deserves an appropriate visual.

Example

Narration:

> Germany possessed vast coal reserves.

Poor visual:

- Hitler speaking

Better visual:

- Coal mines
- Ruhr industrial region
- Coal extraction
- Steel factories

The visual should reinforce the spoken information.

---

## Principle 3 — One Idea Per Shot

Every shot should communicate exactly one idea.

Avoid combining multiple concepts into a single visual.

Bad

```
Germany
Coal
Oil
Hitler
Map
Explosion
Factory
```

Good

Shot 1

Germany map

Shot 2

Coal mine

Shot 3

Factory

Shot 4

Oil barrels

Each shot communicates one idea.

---

## Principle 4 — Historical Accuracy

Whenever factual content is discussed:

Prefer authentic media.

Priority:

1. Public domain archives
2. Historical photographs
3. Maps
4. Stock footage
5. AI-generated media

Generated media should be the final fallback.

---

## Principle 5 — AI is Creative, Not Authoritative

AI proposes.

Humans approve.

Creative decisions may be generated by AI.

Factual correctness should be verifiable.

---

## Principle 6 — Motion Has Purpose

Camera movement should communicate meaning.

Examples:

Slow zoom

- emphasis
- seriousness

Fast push

- urgency
- excitement

Pan

- exploration

Pull back

- reveal

Avoid unnecessary movement.

Motion should support storytelling.

---

## Principle 7 — Cinematic Continuity

Consecutive shots should feel connected.

Maintain consistency in:

- color
- lighting
- historical era
- framing
- pacing

Avoid visual whiplash.

---

## Principle 8 — Reuse Before Generate

Generating AI media is expensive.

Before generation, search for:

- Project assets
- Public domain archives
- Government archives
- Wikimedia Commons
- Stock footage
- Previous generations

Only generate when suitable media cannot be found.

---

## Principle 9 — Every Shot Has Intent

Every shot must have a reason.

Examples

Introduce

Explain

Compare

Reveal

Contrast

Emphasize

Transition

If intent cannot be identified,

the shot should be redesigned.

---

## Principle 10 — Visual Variety

Avoid repeating:

- same framing
- same angle
- same asset type

Mix:

- maps
- portraits
- documents
- infographics
- video
- photographs
- AI scenes

Maintain viewer engagement.

---

## Principle 11 — Emotional Progression

Videos should build emotional momentum.

Typical progression:

Hook

↓

Curiosity

↓

Discovery

↓

Conflict

↓

Resolution

↓

Takeaway

The pacing should match the emotional arc.

---

## Principle 12 — Cognitive Simplicity

The viewer should never struggle to understand a shot.

Avoid:

- clutter
- excessive text
- multiple focal points

Each frame should communicate one clear message.

---

## Principle 13 — Documentary Style

The default visual language is documentary.

Preferred assets:

- historical photos
- satellite imagery
- maps
- newspapers
- archival footage
- government records
- diagrams

Not:

- flashy animations
- meme edits
- excessive VFX

---

## Principle 14 — Editing Should Feel Invisible

The viewer should notice the story,

not the editing.

Transitions should support pacing,

not call attention to themselves.

---

## Principle 15 — Quality Over Quantity

A strong 3-second shot is better than three weak shots.

Do not generate visuals merely to fill time.

---

# Visual Asset Strategy

Preferred order of acquisition

```text
Existing Project Assets
            ↓
Historical Archives
            ↓
Public Domain
            ↓
Stock Media
            ↓
AI Video
            ↓
AI Image
            ↓
Image Animation
```

The engine should always move downward through this hierarchy.

---

# Shot Philosophy

A Shot is **not media**.

A Shot is a creative instruction.

Example

```yaml
intent:
  Explain Germany's coal advantage

duration:
  3 seconds

camera:
  slow_zoom

strategy:
  historical_search

fallback:
  generate_image

transition:
  dissolve
```

The renderer executes the shot.

The shot does not contain the rendered media.

---

# Camera Language

Recommended defaults

| Intent | Camera |
|----------|---------|
| Reveal | Pull Back |
| Explain | Static |
| Tension | Slow Push |
| Discovery | Pan |
| Comparison | Split Frame |
| Importance | Slow Zoom |

---

# Asset Selection Philosophy

Every asset receives a confidence score.

Factors include

- relevance
- quality
- historical accuracy
- licensing
- resolution

The planner should choose the highest-confidence asset.

---

# AI Generation Philosophy

AI-generated assets should be visually consistent with surrounding media.

Generated content should never feel disconnected from archival or stock assets.

Consistency is more important than novelty.

---

# Human Review Philosophy

Humans review:

- timeline
- scene order
- shot quality
- factual correctness

Humans should not need to manually edit individual frames.

The engine should automate execution, not creativity.

---

# Non-Goals

This engine is not intended to:

- replace professional filmmakers
- fabricate historical evidence
- maximize AI generation
- create random visual effects

Its purpose is to accelerate high-quality video production.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful video should satisfy the following:

✓ Every sentence has meaningful visual reinforcement

✓ Visuals are historically accurate where applicable

✓ Camera movement has purpose

✓ Assets are reused before generated

✓ Viewer attention is maintained

✓ Editing feels natural

✓ Narrative remains the primary focus

---

# Philosophy Summary

The Video Generation Engine follows one central belief.

> **AI should think like a director, plan like a producer, and execute like an editor.**

Every architectural decision in this repository should reinforce that philosophy.
